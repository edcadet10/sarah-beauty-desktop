# Close or reopen a book period

Closing retains the reviewed financial reports and locks changes dated through the period end. Use the closing review to resolve missing facts before approval. A confirmed zero is different from an unknown opening.

## Close a reviewed period

1. Complete the actual opening balances and review any unknown asset or loan information shown in the report warnings.
2. Complete an end-of-period reconciliation for each relevant cash, bank, and processor account. Use the actual account evidence. Explain real outstanding items and any documented bank errors.
3. Open **Book periods**. Enter the period start and end, closing reason, and review evidence. The first period must include the earliest recorded financial date. Later periods start the day after the current closed-through date.
4. Select **Review book period closing**. Resolve every listed opening, reconciliation, report, or date issue. Balanced totals alone do not resolve missing inputs.
5. Expand **Captured financial reports**. Review profit and loss, balance sheet, trial balance, cash movements, customer aging, supplier balances, tax activity, and supporting entries. These tables use the captured record version.
6. Confirm your owner passphrase and select **Close reviewed book period**. The app saves the report snapshot and date lock together. If business records changed after the review, refresh and review again.

The saved period appears under **Retained book-period reports**. It identifies the owner, time, reason, evidence, record version, and supporting reconciliations. Reopening does not replace these reports.

## Reopen and revise a period

1. Open the saved period in history. Reopen any later closed book periods first.
2. Enter the reason, confirm your passphrase, and select **Reopen book period**. The previous closed-through date is restored and the original reports remain available.
3. If changing a cash date, explicitly reopen the affected bank reconciliation too. Book-period reopening leaves these account snapshots protected.
4. Record the supported correction, complete any revised reconciliations, and review the period again.
5. Close the revised period. The new report set has its own snapshot identity, time, and record version; the original remains marked **Reopened**.

Report snapshots are management records under the app's selected cash-method policy. Closing a book period does not mark a tax return filed.
