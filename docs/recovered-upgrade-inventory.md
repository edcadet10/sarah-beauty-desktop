# Activate an upgraded recovery copy without losing its record checks

Activation now compares every original table/count and permits only known empty tables introduced after the archive's schema. Audit and recovery-metadata increases remain explicit. Unexpected/missing tables, extra rows in a newly introduced table, changed financial totals, and altered files still prevent activation. Additive migrations must update the small introduced-table inventory deliberately; a different table count alone cannot establish changed business data.

Two real encrypted restore → prepare → activate checks failed before this change for schemas 31 and 35. Their older-layout fixtures now remove later tables/indexes only after confirming those tables are empty; they no longer retain modern tables under an older version label. The verified staged databases stay at their original schema while their writable copies migrate.

All four new migration cases also pass with stronger monetary and rejection checks: genuine older layouts preserve an actual posted 12,345-cent receipt, balanced journal, source identity/history, and expected preparation/activation watermark; unexpected tables and nonempty newly added ownership tables are rejected without publishing an active pointer. These are local cryptographic recovery checks, not a second-computer rehearsal.
