# Review owner activity and balances

1. Open **Reports** and choose **Owner schedule**.
2. Enter the period start and end/as-of date, then select **Run report**.
3. Expand **Owner period activity**, **Owner posted balances**, and **Loan owner attribution**. Each table uses pages of 100 records.
4. Choose a **Supporting owner** and select **Show captured owner balances and sources**. Review each account's opening, debits, credits, and closing, then expand the supporting entries.

Contributions, draws, reimbursement payables, and loans remain separate. Amounts follow approved source identities. Ownership percentages do not split every receipt, expense, or withdrawal. A purchase's personal benefit follows the recorded benefiting owner; owner-funded business costs exclude the personal share.

Period contributions and draws exclude evidenced opening entries. The account roll-forward includes those entries. Payable added and cleared includes opening adjustments and reversals. Reimbursements identify the approved repayment of owner-funded costs; they do not create another expense. All period measures include their dated reversals.

Posted owner balances describe approved ledger entries. They do not certify earlier unrecorded amounts, tax capital, tax basis, or an entitlement to withdraw money. Inactive owners remain visible in their financial history.

A loan's closing principal is included in an owner's confirmed attributed subtotal only when every supporting principal entry through the as-of date captures that same owner lender. Older missing attribution stays unconfirmed, even if a later repayment captures an owner. Other loans remain in the separate attribution table; current register links do not fill history. See [Retain a loan's lender identity](loan-lenders.md).

The schedule shares a consistent database snapshot with the other reports. New book-period closings retain it. Earlier closing reports without this schedule display that it was not captured rather than loading today's records.
