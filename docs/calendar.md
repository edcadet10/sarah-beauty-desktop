# Review the calendar

1. Open **Calendar**. Choose Day, Week, Month, or Date range.
2. Enter the start date. Week uses Monday through Sunday; Month uses the complete month containing that date. Date range also requires an end date.
3. Enter the display time zone, choose an artist or All artists, and decide whether to include cancelled work.
4. Select **Load calendar**. Check the captured range, zone, artist, cancellation filter, and watermark above the results. Changing filters does not change an already captured result.
5. Select a date, then an appointment. Its source details show the booking and appointment IDs, artist, actual recipient places, original stated times and zone, location, issues, and recorded override reason.

An overnight appointment appears on each occupied date. An appointment ending exactly at midnight does not occupy the following day. Changing the display zone can move work to another date; its original stated times remain available in the source details.

Expand **All scheduled appointments in this window** to review the complete captured selection. Date grids show at most 42 dates at a time; appointment and event lists show at most 100 records per page. Use the earlier/later actions for more results.

## Review undecided work

**Untimed appointments** includes work with incomplete times across all dates in the selected artist filter. These entries do not gain invented midnight slots. **Event date undecided** keeps bookings without a known date separate.

**Event dates** uses each event's own recorded local date and zone. It identifies whether relevant event-day work is scheduled; a dated booking alone is not an appointment.

An overlap remains visible when a scheduling override has been recorded. Review the reason and related source IDs. Past planned work stays planned until its actual status is recorded. Calendar review creates no payments or income. Use **Bookings** to edit the source, then load the calendar again.
