# Review tax activity and filing records

1. Open **Reports** and choose **Tax activity**.
2. Enter the financial period start and end/as-of date, then select **Run report**.
3. Review charged, collected, refunded, remitted, recoverable, and liability amounts using their stated date bases.
4. Expand **Captured tax filing periods**. Choose a **Supporting captured tax period** and select **Show captured filing review history** to review its decisions, reported amounts, references, evidence, owner, and time.

Filing reviews describe the registered records at report capture time. Their filing dates can fall after the financial as-of date. The reporting-period filter selects registered tax periods that overlap the financial window, including quarters and archived periods. Unknown due dates and missing reported amounts remain explicit.

Remittances do not mark returns filed. A filing or amendment does not create a tax payment, recovery, or journal adjustment. A reported zero stays separate from an unrecorded amount. Each amendment retains the original filing and its reported amount; it does not replace that history.

New book-period closings retain these filing reviews alongside the tax figures. Later decisions do not change the captured report. An older closing report without filing reviews displays that they were not captured rather than loading today's status. Only registered periods are listed; add confirmed periods in **Tax periods** when needed.
