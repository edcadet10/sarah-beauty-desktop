# Reserve received value as customer credit

1. Open **Customer credits**, choose **Issue funded credit**, and select **Prepare credit action**.
2. Record the explicit decision, effective date, amount, and original approved receipt.
3. Select **Save for review**. Review the payer, source classification, included tax, and remaining receipt and credit value.
4. Confirm your local owner passphrase and select **Approve credit action**.

The credit reserves its original receipt value. That reserved share cannot also be spent as unused receipt value. Creating a credit records no additional cash or income. An identified unclassified receipt can fund a credit; an unknown payer must first be identified through a supported reviewed classification rather than an invented customer.

## Keep held money separate from income

Credits funded by held customer deposits or identified suspense retain that treatment. They cannot pay an invoice while their source remains held or unclassified.

To recognize an agreed share, explicitly return that unused credit share to receipt availability, approve its reviewed classification under **Money**, and issue a credit from that classification. This recognizes only the reviewed amount and creates no additional cash receipt. Use **Apply payments** to apply the resulting income-funded credit.

For an actual refund from unused credit, prepare a Refund under **Money** and select both its original receipt and linked funded credit. Keep the source payer and classification; enter the actual amount, account, date, tax treatment, and evidence. Approval releases the credit funding and refunds its original receipt together. A future reversal preserves the linked history.
