# Review registered asset cost and tax basis

Registered cost and tax basis describe the asset. Approved book cost, depreciation, and carrying value come from their financial entries. Reviewing register facts creates no expense, deduction, cash movement, or tax filing.

1. Save and select the asset under **Resources → Assets**. Complete other unsaved asset fields first.
2. Expand **Reviewed registered asset facts**. Enter the reviewed cost and tax basis. Leave an unknown amount blank; enter zero only when explicitly confirmed.
3. Record the actual reason and supporting evidence. Select **Review registered asset facts**.
4. Compare the captured previous and proposed amounts. Enter your local owner passphrase and select **Approve registered asset facts**. Changed fields or a changed asset require a fresh review.
5. Expand **Registered asset fact history** to inspect the previous values, new values, evidence, reviewer, and time.

After book entries or a fact review exist, use this reviewed action for cost and tax-basis changes. Ordinary register editing cannot overwrite their history. The approved ledger remains unchanged; any correction to book cost, depreciation, or business use needs its financial workflow. Later fact reviews retain earlier decisions, including an explicit return to an unconfirmed value.
