# Keep LIVE creation separate from operational readiness

The pure creation policy requires explicit USD currency, business time zone, books start date and new-business-only acknowledgment. It preserves unknown legal identity/reviews, EIN, ownership and opening facts. It creates no workspace or entries itself.

The separate operational policy requires a legal name and explicit completed formation/EIN/banking/tax reviews. Confirmed and NotApplicable are recorded owner assessments with private reasons; these checks do not verify registration or determine legal/tax treatment. Pending and Unconfirmed stay blocking rather than becoming zero or confirmed by default.

An operational date must fall from the reviewed books start through today in the business time zone. Earlier-business amounts need the explicit opening workflow. A supplied instant supports deterministic policy tests without changing Windows time. Runtime enforcement must supply its own current clock.

Thirty-one policy/profile cases pass, including missing scope/settings/reviews, preserved unknowns, before-start/future-date rejection and a midnight UTC/Eastern boundary. This increment defines the contract only. Transactional entry guards, clean creation, native mode selection and LIVE release acceptance follow separately.
