# Review the debt schedule

1. Open **Reports** and choose **Debt schedule**.
2. Enter the period start and end/as-of date, then select **Run report**.
3. Expand **Loan principal balances** and **Loan period activity**. Each table uses pages of 100 records.
4. Choose a **Supporting loan** and select **Show captured loan facts and sources**. Review current register facts separately from its captured lender history and supporting entries.

The schedule also appears under **Captured financial reports** in a retained book-period review. Older report versions without the schedule display that it was not captured.

The debt schedule captures individual loan principal at the start and end of the selected period. It separates approved opening adjustments, new proceeds, principal repayments, other principal changes, interest, and fees. Period amounts include their dated reversals. Principal never becomes ordinary income or expense.

A registered opening amount does not replace an approved loan opening. Without approved principal entries through the as-of date, the posted principal is unknown. The current register facts remain visible separately, including a registered zero. Once approved entries fully repay a loan, its posted closing principal is known zero. A loan first recorded within the period can have a zero posted opening because no earlier approved entries exist.

Archived loans remain in financial history. Current register names, terms, due dates, evidence, and owner links describe the register at report capture time. Supporting entries preserve movement identities and their captured lender facts. An older movement without captured lender attribution remains unconfirmed; today's owner link does not fill its history. See [Retain a loan's lender identity](loan-lenders.md).

The schedule shares a consistent database snapshot with the trial balance and other reports. New period closes retain its version and facts. Older saved reports without a debt schedule must explicitly display that it was not captured; they must not load newer records.
